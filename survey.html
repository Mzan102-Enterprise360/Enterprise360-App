Your experience matters

We want to understand what helps small businesses operate successfully, especially after receiving funding, machinery, equipment or business-development support.

Independent, voluntary research. Approximately 5 minutes. No membership, payment, name, phone number or business registration number is required. This survey is not run on behalf of Kumba, Khumani, SIOC-CDT or any other funder. Your participation does not affect funding decisions.

Privacy and consent

Mzan 102 (Pty) Ltd, through MzanConnect, will use your answers to prepare summary research findings about local SMMEs. Responses are stored with a hosted database provider and may be processed outside South Africa. We do not request personal names or contact details. In public reports, small groups will be combined or omitted to reduce the risk of identifying participants. Results are not shared as individual business records with sponsors. Responses will be retained only as long as necessary for the research, with a planned maximum of 12 months after survey closure. For research privacy questions, email reception.mzan102@gmail.com. As no identifying details are collected, we may not be able to locate and remove an individual answer after submission.

Please do not share anyone else's confidential information. You can stop before submission. Select only answers you feel comfortable sharing; “prefer not to say” is available for sensitive questions.
