Your experience matters

We want to understand what helps small businesses operate successfully, especially after receiving funding, machinery, equipment or business-development support.

Independent, voluntary research. Approximately 5–7 minutes. No paid membership or payment is required. No personal name or business registration number is requested. Contact details are needed only if you voluntarily request follow-up. This survey is not run on behalf of Kumba, Khumani, SIOC-CDT or any other funder. Your participation does not affect funding decisions.

Privacy and consent

Mzan 102 (Pty) Ltd, through MzanConnect, organises this independent research. Responses will be used to prepare summary findings about local SMME challenges. Survey data is hosted by a database service provider and may be processed outside South Africa. We do not share identifiable survey records with funders or sponsors; small groups will be combined or omitted from public reports.

The final Yes/No question lets you express interest in voluntary business monitoring and support follow-up. If you select Yes, we request an email address or WhatsApp number and separate consent to contact you about this research or relevant business monitoring/support opportunities only. Selecting No does not require contact details. Neither answer creates a paid MzanConnect membership or guarantees services or funding. We will not use this permission for unrelated marketing.

We plan to keep responses for no longer than 12 months after survey closure. You may ask us to stop contacting you, or request access, correction or deletion of contact information, at reception.mzan102@gmail.com. Without identifying information, we may be unable to identify a previously submitted anonymous response. Participation is voluntary and does not affect funding decisions. Please do not disclose anyone else's confidential information.
