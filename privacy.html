Effective date: 13 November 2026. This notice explains how personal information is handled through MzanConnect. It does not itself certify legal compliance.

Our commitment

We aim to protect members' personal and business information and handle it responsibly under South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 (POPIA). We do not sell member information or share it for unrelated marketing without a lawful basis. Confidentiality is important, but information may need to be processed by authorised service providers or disclosed when required by law.

Information we may process

Depending on which features you use, this may include your name, contact details, login email, business profile, compliance checklist responses, bookkeeping entries, membership and payment records, and support messages. Do not submit passwords, bank PINs or unnecessary sensitive documents in support messages.

Why we use it

We process information to create and secure accounts, provide member features, maintain business records requested by members, respond to support requests, manage billing and receipts, and protect the service from misuse. We should collect only information reasonably necessary for these purposes.

Who may handle information

Access should be restricted to authorised MzanConnect administrators and necessary technology or payment providers acting for the service, subject to appropriate safeguards. Current services may include Supabase for authentication and data storage, Brevo for transactional email, and Yoco for payments. External government and supplier websites have their own privacy practices. Information may also be disclosed when legally required.

Security and retention

We use account authentication and access controls, and seek to apply appropriate technical and organisational safeguards. No online system can guarantee absolute security. Information should be retained only as long as needed for service, accounting, dispute resolution or legal requirements, and then securely deleted or de-identified where appropriate. Actual controls and retention arrangements must be reviewed regularly.

Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you may request access to your personal information, correction of inaccurate details, or deletion or restriction where appropriate. You may object to certain processing and raise concerns about how information is used. Requests may require identity verification and some records must be retained by law.

Contact and complaints

For privacy questions or a personal-information request, contact MzanConnect through the Member Support page if you have an account. If you cannot access your account, contact Mzan 102 (Pty) Ltd through its published business contact channel. You may also contact the Information Regulator of South Africa.

Updates

This notice may be revised as features and service providers change. Material changes should be communicated to members where required.
